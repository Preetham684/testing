# PocketPilot

**Before you spend, Cubbie shows what it costs you today and what it costs your future.**

A Cubbie Skill for the Cubbie Hackathon (Track 1). PocketPilot has two halves. Text Cubbie your goal and a purchase, and it tells you:

- **Runway:** your safe-to-spend number today
- **Future Fund:** how much the purchase pushes back your goal date

No spreadsheet. No bank login. Just text.

## Example

> **You:** Saving $12,000 for grad school in 3 years. I have $1,500 and can save $250/month.
> **Cubbie:** You'll reach $10,500, $1,500 short. Save $292/month to hit it.
>
> **You:** What if I buy a $300 concert ticket?
> **Cubbie:** Pushes your grad date back about 5 weeks.

## Install

Text this folder's GitHub link to Cubbie.

## Notes

All numbers come from `scripts/calc.py`. Projections are illustrations, not advice.
